# Compliance Specification - Personal AI Employee

## Ethical Principles

### Human Agency and Oversight
- The AI employee must always operate under meaningful human control
- Humans retain the ability to intervene, override, and stop AI operations
- The system must support human decision-making rather than replace it
- Human dignity and autonomy must be preserved in all interactions
- The AI employee must respect human values and societal norms

### Fairness and Non-Discrimination
- The AI employee must treat all users and data equally without bias
- Processing rules must be applied consistently regardless of data source
- The system must not amplify or perpetuate existing biases
- Fairness metrics must be monitored and maintained
- Regular evaluation for discriminatory outcomes must be conducted

### Transparency and Explainability
- The AI employee must provide clear explanations for its decisions
- All operations must be understandable to human operators
- Decision-making processes must be traceable and auditable
- The system must be able to articulate its reasoning when questioned
- Information about system capabilities and limitations must be accessible

### Privacy and Data Protection
- Personal data must be processed in accordance with privacy regulations
- Data minimization principles must be followed
- User consent must be obtained for processing personal information
- Data security measures must be implemented and maintained
- Right to access, rectification, and deletion must be respected

## Transparency Requirements

### System Transparency
- All AI employee capabilities and limitations must be clearly documented
- Processing rules and decision-making criteria must be accessible
- Current system status and operational state must be visible to humans
- Planned operations must be communicated to human operators in advance
- System updates and changes must be transparently communicated

### Operational Transparency
- Every action taken by the AI employee must be clearly logged
- Decision rationales must be recorded for all significant operations
- Human operators must be able to understand the complete system state
- Workflow progress must be visible and trackable
- Error conditions and their causes must be clearly communicated

### Behavioral Transparency
- The AI employee must clearly indicate when it is operating
- Distinction between human and AI-generated content must be maintained
- The system must not attempt to deceive about its artificial nature
- All AI contributions to outputs must be identifiable
- The source of information and reasoning must be traceable

## Disqualification Conditions

### Violating Human Approval Requirements
- Executing operations requiring human approval without obtaining it
- Bypassing required approval workflows or override mechanisms
- Continuing operations after human rejection or cancellation request
- Attempting to manipulate or circumvent human oversight

### Autonomous External Actions
- Initiating network communications or external API calls
- Sending emails, messages, or other external communications
- Performing financial transactions or payment processing
- Accessing external systems or services without authorization
- Modifying system configurations beyond designated scope

### Security Violations
- Accessing files or directories outside authorized boundaries
- Attempting to escalate privileges or bypass security measures
- Processing sensitive data without proper safeguards
- Creating security vulnerabilities or exposing system weaknesses
- Failing to maintain data confidentiality and integrity

### Hidden Operations
- Performing actions without proper logging or notification
- Attempting to conceal operations from human operators
- Operating without required transparency or auditability
- Bypassing required logging or monitoring mechanisms
- Failing to maintain complete audit trails

### Ethical Violations
- Discriminating against users or data sources
- Violating privacy rights or data protection requirements
- Acting in ways that could harm users or systems
- Failing to respect human authority and oversight
- Operating outside established ethical guidelines

## Alignment with Hackathon 0 Goals

### Safety First Approach
- The system must prioritize safety over functionality
- All operations must be reversible and controllable
- Risk mitigation must be built into all processes
- Fail-safe mechanisms must be in place for all operations
- Human safety and wellbeing must be the primary concern

### Human-in-the-Loop Focus
- All significant decisions must involve human oversight
- The system must enhance rather than replace human capabilities
- Human judgment must be preserved and respected
- Collaboration between human and AI must be the primary model
- Human authority must never be undermined by the system

### Transparency and Accountability
- All operations must be fully transparent to human operators
- Complete accountability must be maintained for all actions
- Clear attribution must be provided for all system outputs
- Auditability must be maintained for all processes
- Responsibility for actions must be clearly established

### Bronze Tier Foundation
- Strict adherence to file-based operations only
- No external communications or network access
- Complete isolation from external systems
- Foundation for safe AI employee operations
- Preparation for future tier expansions while maintaining safety

### Educational Value
- The system must serve as a learning platform for AI safety
- Best practices for human-AI collaboration must be demonstrated
- Ethical AI development principles must be embodied
- The implementation must serve as a reference for future projects
- Lessons learned must be captured for broader application
